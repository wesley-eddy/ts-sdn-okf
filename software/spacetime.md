# Software Information

Spacetime is a managed platform as a service for developers to orchestrate
networks in motion.  Spacetime can evaluate options and establish physical
links between satellites, aircraft, ships, and ground stations. Spacetime
predicts and manages a network's physical links, including proactive scheduling
and reconstruction ahead of disruptions, instead of only afterwards.
