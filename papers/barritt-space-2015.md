# Publication Background

This is an early paper describing the motivations and concept of TS-SDN, from
authors working in the industry designing air and space networks, and dealing
with the problems of controlling them efficiently.

# Paper Summary

The paper explains typical SDN network architecture for an audience of
aerospace professionals, and describes adapting the SDN approach for air &
space networks, resulting in "temporospatial SDN" (TS-SDN).  Three key
application use cases are outlined, for topology management, packet routing,
and interference avoidance.
