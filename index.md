# Papers

Academic publications on TS-SDN.

* 2015
  - [Barritt/Eddy AIAA SPACE paper](papers/barritt-space-2015.md) - Early paper describing the industry needs and concept of TS-SDN.
* 2016
  - [Barritt/Eddy AIAA ICSSC paper](papers/barritt-icssc-2016.md) - Advantages of SDN over dynamic routing protocols for large LEO satellite constellations.
* 2017
  - [Barritt et al. IEEE Aerospace Conference paper](papers/barritt-aeroconf-2017.md) - Application of TS-SDN to UAV mesh and backhaul networks, comparing it with distributed mesh routing.
  - [Barritt PhD dissertation](papers/barritt-phd-2017.md) - Modeling, simulation, and TS-SDN operational control of aerospace networks, including LEO constellations and HAPS.
* 2022
  - [Barritt/Eddy IEEE Aerospace Conference paper](papers/barritt-aeroconf-2022.md) - TS-SDN-based service management and orchestration for 5G and 6G non-terrestrial networks.
  - [Loon paper from SIGCOMM](papers/uyeda-sigcomm2022.md) - Published results and lessons from controlling an aerospace network via TS-SDN.
